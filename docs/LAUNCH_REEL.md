# Family Room reel: footage plan

**Status:** storyboard only. A feature-demonstration reel requires a real capture from the SwiftUI app on an authorized Apple device. The website's Mantelpiece window and scenic moments are illustrations based on current source and sample data; they are not recordings of the app or private family photos.

## Visual direction

Make the reel feel like opening a family album in late afternoon: cream paper, soft sunlight, terracotta ink, and patient movement between moments. Use the Mantelpiece serif/sans roles and warm palette from `Sources/Shared/DesignSystem.swift`. Keep every piece of UI legible and separate ambient imagery from actual captured app footage.

| Time | Picture and motion | Copy | Source needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | A sunlit cream field resolves into the Family Room mark and album title. | `Family Room` | Original site identity. |
| 5–13 s | A real iPad or Mac capture moves through the Mantelpiece river of sample moments. | `A year, held close.` | Authorized app recording required. |
| 13–22 s | In the same real capture, select a sample person and show the river narrowing. | `Find the days you shared.` | Authorized iPad/Mac recording required; this filter is not demonstrated by the phone home view. |
| 22–31 s | Open a sample memory detail, then hold on its people and place. | `Every moment has a story.` | Authorized app recording required. |
| 31–40 s | Return to the album cover, sunlight settling. End on site address only after hosting is verified. | `Still being made.` | Original motion graphics; verified URL still pending. |

Target 40 seconds at 1920×1080 and 30 fps, with web H.264 MP4, a poster, and editable sources. Music or sound effects need owned or clearly licensed source records; silence is acceptable. Provide captions if narration or meaningful audio is used. The site should use a user-initiated video player once the reel is rendered, visually reviewed, and browser tested.

Do not imply that photo import, automatic face grouping, iCloud sharing, or story export works. The current source uses sample people and moments; its face service returns dummy IDs and its story composer does not export a file.
