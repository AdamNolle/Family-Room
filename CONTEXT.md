# Family Room

Family Room is a private home for photos, videos and shared memories.

## Language

**Room**: A private group whose members share a memory library.

**Member**: Someone with permission to access a Room, potentially through several authorized devices.

**Person**: Someone depicted in a memory, whether or not they are a Member.

**Person merge**: A Room correction that maps one depicted Person profile to the profile kept; existing and future tags appear under the kept profile while original media remains unchanged.

**Moment**: An occasion containing related photos and videos.

**Album**: A curated collection that may span several Moments.

**Story**: A film project and its published movie.

**Storage source**: A connected cloud account, server or device holding copies.

**Video evidence**: Timestamped machine-recognized words or machine-suggested sound/scene clues from a video.

**Exact phrase**: Ordered words found in one video-evidence text stream; the recognition itself can still be mistaken.
**Search context**: Unquoted words that describe a requested video moment around an exact phrase. Search context can help rank nearby evidence but does not make a phrase exact or identify a speaker.

**Album-only library**: The explicitly published contents of one Album, available to viewers independently of the rest of its Room.

**Device revocation**: Removal of a device's permission to receive future Room contributions or publish accepted changes. Previously downloaded memories remain with their recipient.

**Ownership offer**: A signed request from the current Room owner naming the one Member device that may accept ownership succession.

**Owner succession**: A Member's acceptance of ownership, after which that Member becomes the Room owner and the previous owner keeps a lesser Room role.

**Access revision**: An owner-approved change to the devices and roles authorized in a Room.

**Key epoch**: A period during which newly contributed memories use one generation of Room access.

**Local departure**: Leaving a Room on one device forgets that device's active Room keys and connections. It does not change the shared signed membership roster; an owner must revoke the device for Room-wide removal.
