01 / THE ROOM

A room shaped by who's in it.

The current Mantelpiece prototype opens on a river of moments on iPad and Mac. Choose a sample person to focus that view; open a memory to see its people, place, and details.

It is designed to feel more like leafing through a family album than managing a folder.

03 / PROJECT STATUS

A place still being made.

Family Room is an active development project. Its current SwiftUI source contains the Mantelpiece home, person selection, and memory detail views using sample data.

Media import, automatic face grouping, iCloud collaboration, and story export are ideas in the project plans; they are not presented here as working features. There is no public app download linked from this page.
